About me

I'm a Software Engineer passionate about building data infrastructure that scales — the kind that makes teams faster and business decisions sharper.

At the MTA, I've been part of building the analytics platform from the ground up. Today it powers thousands of pipelines daily, replacing legacy manual systems and giving teams access to reliable, real-time data. My work spans ETL design, platform reliability, observability, CI/CD, and cost engineering — the full stack of keeping production data systems healthy.

I hold a Master's in Computer Science from Pace University (3.97 GPA) and spend my off-hours experimenting with platform tools, contributing to open source, and building AI-powered side projects.

When I'm not at the keyboard, you'll find me exploring emerging tech, catching up on anime, playing soccer, or hunting for the perfect cup of coffee ☕
